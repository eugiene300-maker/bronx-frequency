The magazine

About the magazine

Local life. Clear facts. Free to read.

We cover the Bronx through its neighborhoods, cultural life, public spaces and the questions people ask about cannabis policy. The aim is useful reading that earns a place in your day, whether you are planning an outing or trying to understand a headline.

A magazine that keeps growing

Stories live in a searchable archive, organized by subject. New coverage adds to that archive rather than replacing the whole publication. Dated event guides retain their original dates so an old recommendation cannot masquerade as a new one.

What you will find here

Neighborhood news and analysis. Art, music and cultural context. A local attraction guide with practical details. Cannabis public-policy and health-literacy explainers. A conversation desk that checks claims against evidence. The Bronx Frequency podcast explores those threads through the people, places and conversations that shape the borough.

Who stands behind the words

The Editorial Desk is the publication’s organizational byline. The team page identifies the editorial pen names used for its coverage areas. Articles link to their sources and distinguish analysis from firsthand reporting. Individual contributors receive credit for their actual work.

How the magazine is supported

BX Buddiez supports the publication as a disclosed sponsor. Sponsor support does not determine the conclusions of an article. The sponsor page explains the relationship, and the editorial guidelines describe how evidence, corrections and conflicts are handled.

Free to read

There is no article paywall. You can follow new stories through the RSS feed, browse by desk or search the archive. A free magazine can still take its readers’ time seriously.
