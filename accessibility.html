Accessibility

Reading, listening and navigation should be straightforward.

The site uses semantic headings, a skip link, visible keyboard focus and responsive layouts. Article text remains available without JavaScript. Browser zoom can enlarge text without changing the content.

Optional interactions

Search and attraction filters help narrow the archive; the full lists remain readable without scripts. The podcast includes written episode notes and links to listening directories. Transcripts appear when the actual transcript is available. The chosen listening service controls audio playback.

Images

Photographs and illustrations have text alternatives. Credits include the source and relevant licensing information. A visual depiction of a place does not certify that place’s accessibility.

Report a barrier

Identify the page and the task that was difficult, with your browser or assistive technology if relevant. Use the contact page’s feedback options. This statement describes design features, not an independent accessibility certification.
