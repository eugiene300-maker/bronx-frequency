Bronx Frequency · Episode 1

Weed Is Legal. Who Wins? The Bronx Story Behind the Storefront

Legal weed changed the rules. Who gets a foothold in the new market? The first Bronx Frequency episode follows the question from cannabis equity to the neighborhood storefront, with BX Buddiez as a starting point.

Listen on Spotify

Episode notes

Behind the storefront

This episode begins with a Bronx question: when a once-prohibited business becomes legal, who gets to build a future in it? The conversation connects cannabis culture, neighborhood identity and the economics behind a storefront.

The equity question

New York’s cannabis framework puts social equity at the center of its stated goals. That opens a larger conversation about what opportunity means after a license is issued, and what residents should look for beyond an opening-day headline. New York OCM: social equity and the adult-use framework

Why a local business belongs in the conversation

BX Buddiez provides a concrete starting point for a story about a changing borough. Its own account emphasizes local culture and education. This episode explores the wider questions that surround that positioning, rather than claiming that one shop resolves them. BX Buddiez: its account of the business

Keep the conversation going

What would meaningful community benefit look like on your block? More opportunity for local businesses, a stronger cultural life, better public information, or something else? Those are questions worth returning to as the story develops.
