About the show

The Bronx has a frequency all its own. You hear it in the music coming out of a storefront, the debate at the next table, and the stories people tell about their block. Bronx Frequency tunes into the people, places, and conversations that make this borough worth paying attention to.

We explore cannabis culture, neighborhood food finds, music, art, and the local businesses finding their footing. One episode might unpack who benefits from legal weed. Another might follow a neighborhood obsession or introduce you to someone doing something extraordinary a few stops from home. Expect curiosity, humor, and questions that go beyond the promotional pitch.

Whether you’ve lived here your whole life or you’re just getting to know the borough, come ready to discover something, rethink something, and leave with a story worth sharing. Subscribe to Bronx Frequency and get to know the Bronx one conversation at a time.
