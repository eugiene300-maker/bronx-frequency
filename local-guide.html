in the Bronx.

Gardens, galleries, music, history and waterfront air. Find the kind of day you want.

Where are the cannabis-friendly spots?

If you mean a place that explicitly allows cannabis consumption, we have not verified a Bronx venue for this guide. That is a limit of the evidence, not a claim that none exist.

NYC Health says adults may smoke or vape cannabis where tobacco smoking is permitted, with exceptions. It lists parks, restaurants, event spaces and businesses, including dispensaries, among prohibited places. Read the city’s rules.

Browse the places below for art, music, gardens and a good afternoon out. Check the smoking label on each entry before you go.
