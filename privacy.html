The magazine

Privacy policy

How the website handles browsing, weather, podcast links and feedback.

The magazine does not run advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, account registration or a comment database. Search and guide filtering happen in your browser.

Weather

The header requests public forecast data from the National Weather Service for a fixed Bronx location. It does not request your device’s location. The weather provider receives normal technical request information. Forecasts are refreshed periodically while the page is visible, and unavailable data is labeled rather than invented.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request logs and account information under its own policies. These platform systems are separate from the magazine’s code.

Podcast directories

Apple Podcasts and Spotify players load on the podcast page. These services may receive technical browsing information and apply their own cookies, playback and privacy policies. Direct links also open iHeartRadio, Podcast Index and Podcast Addict. Other directory buttons are labeled when they open a search.

Feedback

The contact form currently shows a preview confirmation. It does not send, store or upload your message or email address.

External links

Source and sponsor websites have their own policies. The magazine does not embed social feeds. Its podcast page links to external listening directories. Photographs are hosted with the site, not loaded from social-media accounts.

Browser storage

The magazine’s own scripts do not set cookies or persistent local storage. External websites and hosting access controls may work separately. Privacy questions can be raised using the contact options shown on the contact page.
